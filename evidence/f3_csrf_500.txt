# F3 - Kegagalan CSRF dibalas HTTP 500, bukan 419

## tanpa _csrf
```
POST /profile/name HTTP/1.1
Host: diksy.ilkom.unej.ac.id
Content-Length: 6

name=x
```
HTTP/1.1 500
Content-Type: text/html; charset=utf-8
Body:  Sesi kedaluwarsa | bLecturer &lt;/&gt; bLecturer Tech Sandbox Academic Lab Office Hours D

## _csrf kosong
```
POST /profile/name HTTP/1.1
Host: diksy.ilkom.unej.ac.id
Content-Length: 13

_csrf=&name=x
```
HTTP/1.1 500
Content-Type: text/html; charset=utf-8
Body:  Sesi kedaluwarsa | bLecturer &lt;/&gt; bLecturer Tech Sandbox Academic Lab Office Hours D

## _csrf acak 64 hex
```
POST /profile/name HTTP/1.1
Host: diksy.ilkom.unej.ac.id
Content-Length: 77

_csrf=aaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaa&name=x
```
HTTP/1.1 500
Content-Type: text/html; charset=utf-8
Body:  Sesi kedaluwarsa | bLecturer &lt;/&gt; bLecturer Tech Sandbox Academic Lab Office Hours D

```
email=[EMAIL]&password=x
```
HTTP/1.1 500
